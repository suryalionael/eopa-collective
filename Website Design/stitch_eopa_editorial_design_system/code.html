The Living Mission

We are an artist-led collective cultivating space for theatre makers, dancers, musicians, circus creators, and poets to exchange craft, develop new works, and sustain regional performance practices.

Performance is an embodied craft demanding shared space, resonant rooms, and immediate communion between artists and audiences. Yet across Eastern Ontario, geographic distance between studios often breeds creative isolation. We dismantle the friction of travel and cost by pooling resources, bartering rehearsal halls, and touring collaborative work across our rural and urban centers.

From Brockville to Kingston, Lanark to Ottawa, and Cornwall to the Valley, we believe artists should not need to relocate to Toronto or Montreal to sustain a profound, challenging, and financially viable performance practice. We build regional stamina together.
