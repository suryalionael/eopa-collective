[normal] Where We Stand Today
[normal] The Collective currently operates as an unincorporated association — an informal agreement among members with no filing fees, though also without personal liability protection. We'll move toward incorporating as a non-profit once our membership base grows to a point where it clearly benefits the people we serve.
[normal] We operate in partnership with Aspen Training Centre, which provides student support for running the Collective — meaning your membership fees also help fund real, hands-on training opportunities for the students who help keep things running.

[normal] PERFORMANCE ART
[normal] A separate practice from the performing arts, worth its own space on the site.
[normal] Where the performing arts (theatre, dance, music, opera, circus, spoken word) are about a piece being performed for an audience, performance art is its own fine-art discipline — the artist's live presence, body, and actions are the artwork itself, often shown in a gallery or museum context rather than a stage.
[normal] A performance art piece typically centers five elements: time, space, the artist's body, the artist's presence, and the relationship between artist and audience. It can be scripted or fully improvised, and it can happen anywhere — a gallery, a public square, or somewhere unexpected — for any length of time. The goal isn't a polished show so much as a live reaction: pieces are often tied to the artist's own experience, and to social or political commentary.
[normal] We're carving this out as its own category because folding it into "performing arts" undersells what makes it distinct — it belongs alongside our other categories, not inside one of them.
